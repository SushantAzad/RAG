# Health Insurance Benefits Guide

## Plan Options

The company offers three medical plan tiers through Meridian Health, open for enrollment during the annual open enrollment period in November, with coverage beginning January 1.

### Plan A: High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP)
- Monthly premium (employee only): $45
- Deductible: $2,000 individual / $4,000 family
- Company HSA contribution: $750/year
- Best for: Employees who are generally healthy and want lower premiums

### Plan B: PPO Standard
- Monthly premium (employee only): $145
- Deductible: $750 individual / $1,500 family
- Best for: Employees who want more predictable costs and broader provider access

### Plan C: PPO Plus
- Monthly premium (employee only): $220
- Deductible: $250 individual / $500 family
- Best for: Employees with ongoing medical needs or families with young children

## Dental and Vision

Dental coverage is bundled at no additional cost for employees enrolled in any medical plan. Vision coverage is optional and costs $8/month for employee only, or $22/month for family coverage.

## Dependent Eligibility

Spouses, domestic partners, and children up to age 26 are eligible for coverage regardless of student or marital status. Documentation such as a marriage certificate or birth certificate is required within 30 days of adding a dependent.

## Life Events

Qualifying life events (marriage, birth, adoption, divorce, loss of other coverage) allow benefit elections to be changed outside of open enrollment. Employees have 30 days from the event date to submit changes through the benefits portal.

## Contact

For plan specific questions, contact Meridian Health directly at their member services line, listed on the back of your insurance card. For enrollment issues, contact the Benefits team via the HR portal.
