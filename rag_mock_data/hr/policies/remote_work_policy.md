# Remote Work Policy

**Department:** Human Resources
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2025
**Last Reviewed:** June 2025

## Overview

This policy outlines the guidelines and expectations for employees who work remotely, either on a full time or hybrid basis. The company supports flexible work arrangements as part of its commitment to employee wellbeing and productivity.

## Eligibility

Employees are eligible for remote work arrangements if they meet the following criteria:

- Have completed at least 90 days of employment
- Hold a role that does not require physical presence (e.g., manufacturing, front desk, lab work)
- Have received manager approval
- Maintain a performance rating of "meets expectations" or higher in their most recent review

## Work Arrangements

There are three approved categories of remote work:

1. **Fully Remote** — Employee works from a home office or approved remote location full time. No regular office attendance is required.
2. **Hybrid** — Employee splits time between the office and a remote location, typically 2-3 days in office per week, as agreed with their manager.
3. **Temporary Remote** — Short term remote arrangement for specific circumstances (illness, family care, relocation) not exceeding 90 days without renewal.

## Equipment and Expenses

The company will provide a laptop, monitor, and standard peripherals for remote employees. A one time home office stipend of $500 is available for desk, chair, and lighting purchases. Internet reimbursement is capped at $50 per month with a submitted receipt.

## Core Hours

All remote employees must be available for collaboration between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in their assigned time zone, regardless of their broader schedule flexibility.

## Security Requirements

Remote employees must use company issued VPN software when accessing internal systems, enable full disk encryption on their work device, and avoid using public unsecured WiFi networks for work related tasks without a VPN connection active.

## Review and Termination of Arrangement

Remote work arrangements are reviewed annually alongside performance reviews. The company reserves the right to modify or terminate a remote work arrangement with 30 days written notice if business needs change or performance concerns arise.

## Questions

Direct questions about this policy to your HR business partner or the People Operations team via the internal HR portal.
