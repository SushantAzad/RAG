# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Department:** Human Resources
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2024
**Last Reviewed:** January 2026

## Accrual Structure

PTO accrues monthly based on tenure:

| Years of Service | Annual PTO Days | Monthly Accrual |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2 years | 15 days | 1.25 days |
| 3-5 years | 20 days | 1.67 days |
| 6-9 years | 25 days | 2.08 days |
| 10+ years | 30 days | 2.5 days |

## Carryover Rules

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the following calendar year. Any balance above 5 days as of December 31 will be forfeited unless local law requires otherwise. Employees in California, Colorado, and Montana are subject to unlimited carryover per state law, since PTO is treated as earned wages in those jurisdictions.

## Requesting Time Off

PTO requests should be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance for single day requests, and 30 days in advance for requests exceeding one week. Manager approval is required before time off is considered confirmed.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is tracked separately from general PTO and accrues at 1 day per month, up to a maximum bank of 12 days. Unused sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out upon separation.

## Holidays

The company observes 11 paid holidays annually, published each December for the following calendar year on the company intranet.

## Payout Upon Separation

Unused accrued PTO is paid out at the employee's final base rate upon voluntary or involuntary separation, in accordance with state law. Sick leave is not eligible for payout.

## Unlimited PTO Pilot

As of Q3 2025, employees in the Product and Engineering departments are part of an unlimited PTO pilot program. Under this pilot, there is no fixed accrual cap, but employees are expected to take a minimum of 15 days off per year and must still follow the standard request and approval process.
