# Vendor Service Agreement Summary — CloudHost Infrastructure Services

**Note:** This is a mock summary document for testing purposes, not a real legal contract.

**Parties:** Company Inc. ("Client") and CloudHost Infrastructure Services LLC ("Vendor")
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2025
**Term:** 24 months, with automatic renewal for successive 12 month terms unless either party provides 60 days written notice of non renewal

## Scope of Services

Vendor will provide cloud infrastructure hosting services including compute, storage, and networking resources as detailed in Exhibit A. Vendor is responsible for maintaining infrastructure uptime, security patching of underlying hypervisor and network layers, and 24/7 infrastructure monitoring.

## Service Level Agreement

- Uptime commitment: 99.95% monthly uptime, excluding scheduled maintenance windows
- Scheduled maintenance: maximum 4 hours per month, with 7 days advance notice
- Support response time: Critical issues within 15 minutes, High priority within 1 hour, Standard priority within 8 business hours

## Service Credits

If uptime falls below the committed 99.95% in a given month, Client is entitled to service credits according to the following schedule:

- 99.0% to 99.94% uptime: 10% credit of that month's invoice
- 95.0% to 98.99% uptime: 25% credit of that month's invoice
- Below 95.0% uptime: 50% credit of that month's invoice

## Data Handling and Security

Vendor agrees to maintain SOC 2 Type II certification throughout the term and to provide the current audit report to Client upon request, no more than once per calendar quarter. Vendor will encrypt all data at rest using AES-256 and all data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Vendor will notify Client of any security incident affecting Client data within 72 hours of discovery.

## Payment Terms

Invoices are issued monthly in arrears, net 30 payment terms. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month. Pricing is fixed for the initial 24 month term, with any renewal term pricing subject to a maximum 5% annual increase.

## Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach with a 30 day cure period. Client may terminate for convenience with 90 days written notice, subject to early termination fees as detailed in Exhibit C if terminated within the first 12 months.

## Limitation of Liability

Vendor's total liability under this agreement is capped at the total fees paid by Client in the 12 months preceding the claim, except in cases of gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of confidentiality obligations.
