# General FAQ

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password." You'll receive an email with a reset link valid for 1 hour. If you don't see the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

**Q: How do I change my billing information?**
A: Navigate to Account Settings > Billing. You can update your payment method, billing address, and view past invoices from that page. Changes take effect immediately for future charges.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase for annual plans and within 14 days for monthly plans, provided the account has not exceeded normal usage limits. Contact support with your order number to initiate a refund request.

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription?**
A: Go to Account Settings > Subscription and click "Cancel Subscription." Your access will continue until the end of the current billing period. We do not offer partial refunds for the remaining period unless required by local law.

**Q: How do I export my data?**
A: From Account Settings > Data & Privacy, click "Export All Data." This generates a downloadable archive containing your account data in JSON and CSV formats, typically ready within 15 minutes for most accounts.

**Q: Is my data encrypted?**
A: Yes. All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+ and at rest using AES-256 encryption, consistent with our security practices described in our Trust Center.

**Q: How do I add team members?**
A: Go to Team Settings > Members and click "Invite Member." Enter their email address and select a role (Admin, Editor, or Viewer). They'll receive an invitation email with a link to join.

**Q: What happens to my data if I cancel?**
A: Your data is retained for 90 days after cancellation in case you wish to reactivate your account. After 90 days, it is permanently deleted from our production systems, though it may persist in encrypted backups for up to an additional 30 days before those backups are also purged.
